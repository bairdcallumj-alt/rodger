Roger and His Wishes

A social sciences and literacy resource exploring Rogernomics, economic change, and the big question behind every powerful wish: what does it give, and what does it take away?

Big ideaEconomic choices create winners, losers, opportunities, and consequences.
Text linkLike wishes in stories, reforms can promise transformation but come with a catch.
Student taskRead, compare, judge, and explain whether the wishes were worth it.
